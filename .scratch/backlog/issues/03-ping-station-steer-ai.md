# Enhancement: Ping a Station to steer the AI Operator

Status: needs-triage
Source: raised during [AI Operator behaviour](../../signal-room/issues/08-ai-operator-behaviour.md), 2026-10-17

## Idea

In the slice, the player can't command an AI Operator; Swap is the only steer. This idea adds one key that pings the
Station the player's Operator is facing, giving that Station's job a large score boost on the nearest AI's Job board.
Only worth adding if playtests show players fighting the AI's choices.

## Open questions

- Does a ping expire, or last until the job is done?
- Which AI answers when the Roster has several?
- How it reads on screen (ping marker, AI acknowledgement), and how it maps to a gamepad Seat in slice 2.
