# Enhancement: Decoy Radio as a Shift gimmick

Status: needs-triage
Source: dropped from the slice in [Radio presets & spectrum read](../../signal-room/issues/12-radio-tuning-prototype.md), 2026-10-17

## Idea

A Radio that is always lit and copyable, but whose frequency no Ticket ever names, so its cards transmit for nothing.
The trap is not reading the Ticket. A later Shift could switch it on as a Modifier. Playtesting the spectrum prototype
showed identical decoys were too punishing, so it needs a tell: a garbled tone and a flickering frequency display.
Maybe the decoy role moves between Radios after each correct copy, so it stays unpredictable.

## Open questions

- A decoy is always lit, so it is always a tempting Radio to engage. Engaging it couples the antenna and darkens the
  real traffic, which is the cost. Is that enough, or does it need more?
- Does the Direction Finder Requisition (greys out decoys) come back with it?
